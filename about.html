Our Story

A Commitment to Healthcare Excellence

We are a company founded in 2008 with the aim of supplying and procuring hospital consumable materials within the Turkish Healthcare System. While we operate with our own staff in Ankara, Istanbul, Izmir, and Adana domestically, we ensure the sales and supply of our products through our dealer network in other provinces. At our headquarters in Ankara, all of our employees work with dedication to provide the best possible service to the healthcare professionals we can reach and their patients. As Alpler Hospital Supplies, we believe in the power of medical technology to improve lives. Our greatest goal is to help save as many lives as we can reach by supplying the products necessary in healthcare services.

We offer our comprehensive healthcare solutions to our country with our highest quality products and experienced staff. Starting out on this journey by selling consumables for anesthesia and intensive care devices, we are today among Turkey's leading medical device companies. With a wide product range, our company focuses on consumables for Anesthesia and Intensive Care, Monitor and Incubator devices, vital monitors, closed-circuit aspiration systems, infusion pumps, syringe pumps, and pain pumps. Our products reach many people every year, supporting our mission of relieving pain, improving health, and extending life expectancy. While closely following all innovations and developments in these fields worldwide, we strive to find the most suitable solutions and products for the institutions we serve. Our greatest goal, through our partnerships with leading global brands such as GE Healthcare, Becton Dickinson (BD), and Avanos/Halyard, is to deliver the highest quality products to those in need at the most competitive prices and in the shortest time possible.

In addition to import and sales, we also provide after-sales service to our customers, with customer satisfaction as our guiding principle. With our deep knowledge base and experienced staff, our company stands ready at all times to meet all the needs of our industry.
